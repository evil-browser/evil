# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about the code, not about the person writing it. Assume the
other party is competent and acting in good faith until you have real evidence
otherwise — and "they disagreed with me" is not evidence.

## Specifically

**Fine:** strong technical disagreement, saying a patch is wrong and why,
rejecting a contribution, asking someone to redo work, pointing out that a claim
in the docs is false.

**Not fine:** insults, harassment, sexualised language or imagery, dragging
someone's identity into a technical argument, publishing private information,
following someone across threads to keep at them, or the well-worn move of
wrapping any of the above in "just being honest".

Disagreement is the point of review. Contempt isn't.

## Scope

Applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any space where
someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report to `conduct@evil.st`. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. Responses run from a private word, through a public correction, to
a temporary or permanent ban, depending on severity and on whether it is a
pattern. Maintainers are held to this at least as strictly as everyone else — if
a maintainer is the problem, say so, and it will be handled by the others.
